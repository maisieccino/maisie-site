It’s been a weird few days! I’m still working through post-covid symptoms (it’s been seven weeks as of writing). This, uh, kinda limits the amount of stuff I can really do. A bunch of time last week was dedicated to running the gamut of tests to try and work out why my body isn’t working quite how it should be. Mostly came up empty other than a recommendation to take B12 supplements. Ah well.

In the meantime, I picked up a fancy lil armband that promises to help me manage my symptoms and pace my energy throughout the day. The idea is that, by being able to conserve my energy, it should help lessen symptoms and help me be able to do the things that matter. Seeing friends. Doing hobbies. Being there for my partner.

The first few days have been a bit annoying! I’ll get a little ping on my phone telling me to slow down and rest if I can, while I’m in the shower or walking to the local cafe or doing the dishes. It’s quite eye-opening how much my heart rate spikes up doing mundane tasks. I hope that by collecting all this data, it’ll guide me in my recovery, and also be of use to any doctor trying to help.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/38/b8387400-0111-40f7-ba76-7e7292e1107b/content/images/2025/07/38F2E29B-5242-4BF4-A376-BD5C1AE23CA3_1_102-1-1.jpeg)

I think, unfortunately for post Covid issues, all GPs can really offer is counselling, ruling out serious issues, and recommend rest and hydration and gradual return to exercise. So I’m trying to get ahead on these things as much as I can. I’ll see what else is available to me and just try and commit fully to trying to get back to the things I love, like dancing and cooking and climbing. Watch this space!

## What I’ve been listening to

### Floating Points - Cascade

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/38/b8387400-0111-40f7-ba76-7e7292e1107b/content/images/2025/07/a3720949005_16-1.jpg)

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/38/b8387400-0111-40f7-ba76-7e7292e1107b/content/images/2025/07/a3192368960_16-1.jpg)

[Link](https://jazzsticks.bandcamp.com/album/this-is-me?ref=blog.mbell.dev)

### Caribou - Suddenly

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/38/b8387400-0111-40f7-ba76-7e7292e1107b/content/images/2025/07/a0092738776_16-1.jpg)

### KOAN Sound - Evocation

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/38/b8387400-0111-40f7-ba76-7e7292e1107b/content/images/2025/07/a0607660249_10-1.jpg)

## And finally… nerd stuff

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/b8/38/b8387400-0111-40f7-ba76-7e7292e1107b/content/images/2025/07/91A306C3-3EB0-4C5A-B379-CB36A87A288C_1_105_c.jpeg)

I’m starting to rebuild my Neovim configuration! Neovim is a text editor that runs in your terminal. I use it at home and work to write code, type up notes, and also review changes. I love the cluttered, distraction free writing experience.

Okay.

Maybe it’s a little busy and I have installed a lot of plugins. But. I like the idea of being able to customise my tools so much that they are unmistakably *mine*! I think there’s something quite wonderful about choosing every keyboard shortcut, menu, feature you want to include. This text editor works my way and my way only. Pure Maisiecore.
