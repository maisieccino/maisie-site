There were a few albums I kept coming back to this year which I just really enjoyed throughout. These are them.

Don't expect any fancy review here, haha. I'm just going to drop a favourite track from each album.

### Everything Everything - Raw Data Feel

Honestly one of my favourite records to come from the boys in recent years, especially thematically.

[Everything Everything - I Want A Love Like This (Official Video)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/mgZ5Bcvnb6Y?feature=oembed)

### Moderat - MORE D4TA

I feel like this is one of the most consistent albums I've listened to in a while, feels like each track flows really well into the next.

[NEON RATS](https://www.youtube.com/embed/DZtnajuFB6s?feature=oembed)

### Editors - EBM

The production quality on this is sublime, it's such a cool and dark album, Editors absolutely nailed it.

[Editors - Heart Attack](https://www.youtube.com/embed/HSDDiEp1YEk?feature=oembed)

### Bonobo - Fragments

Personally I preferred *Migration&#x20;*&#x74;o this album, but this album has some really fantastic moments, like the drop in *Otomo*.

[Bonobo - Otomo (feat. O’Flynn) (Official Audio)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/MLP487SE2Mg?feature=oembed)

### Ibibio Sound Machine - Electricity

This album is such a 90s vibe and the production quality is incredible, it's so much fun. I also just learned it was produced by Hot Chip, that's really cool.

[Ibibio Sound Machine - All That You Want (Official Audio)](https://www.youtube.com/embed/h7kBazdEQJw?feature=oembed)
