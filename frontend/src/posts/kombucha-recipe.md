My usual Kombucha recipe, updated as the recipe is updated.

Version 1.1, released 2020/02/03

**Update: I managed to kill the Kombucha. Rest in peace buddy.**

## Sweet Tea

* 50g demerera sugar
* 150g golden granulated sugar
* 2L hot (not boiling) water
* 8tsp assam black tea
* 2tsp sencha green tea

Add to \~200ml starter culture.

## First Fermentation

Leave for \~2 weeks.

## Second Fermentation

Add flavourings + 1tsp golden granulated sugar if necesasry, leave for 2-3 days.

## Flavourings

Lime & Ginger - dash of lime juice, and some chopped ginger.

Rosemary & Citrus - a couple of rosemary sprigs, some orange zest, and a dash of lemon juice.

Mango & Passion Fruit - a liberal dash of "mango, passion fruit and yuzu couli" from Waitrose.

Apple & Cinammon - small amount of diced apple (no skin), and a cinammon stick.

Chili & Lime: This was ok, the lime seemed to stop the kombucha from carbonating very well. The chili was subtle but nice.

Lavender & Honey: was petrified that we were growing botulism, 2/10
