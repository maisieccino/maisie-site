Specialty coffee shop in Haggerston. Always has good batch brew on, and some cracking toasties.

The cafe is a really lovely place to have a seat, watch the world go by and do some writing.

Space is sometimes at a premium because it's often used as a co-working spot, so you might have to sit outside or takeaway. Or, be brave and share a table with a fellow coffee enjoyer. :)
