## The short version

In case your attention span has been ruined by LLMs.

I hate generative AI and will never use it for my personal work.

## The less short version

<!-- > [!info] -->
<!-- > I have updated this page on 2026-08-10 to reflect my experiences using this technology. -->

LLMs and generative AI is a fundamentally evil technology. It is built on stolen art and creativity, and trained using exploited global majority labour. It tries badly to replace the work of artists with low quality, generated slop.

AI usage has documented negative effects on [mental health](https://www.cureus.com/articles/504063-psychosis-in-the-age-of-large-language-models-llms-a-narrative-review-of-the-proposed-construct-of-ai-induced-psychosis.pdf), attention span and [problem solving skills](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6442979).

As a result, I do not use these tools in my personal life or projects. Even if they were to improve productivity (they don't), I still will not use these tools.

My views are shared really well in Anthony Moser's post [I Am An AI Hater](https://anthonymoser.github.io/writing/ai/haterdom/2025/08/26/i-am-an-ai-hater.html).

I have tried out LLM-based workflows for professional development and I do not find it speeds up my work enough to be useful.
